## Start with the numbers you know

Adding a service can create a potential source of revenue, but the result depends on the practice. Estimate demand using your own patient population, available appointments, and proposed pricing rather than assuming a specific return.

## Account for the full cost

Include operating fees, staff time, training, space, supplies, marketing, and any other applicable costs. Ask for current written terms, and consider how a new service would affect the capacity of your existing operations.

## Evaluate clinical fit too

Financial planning should sit alongside clinical appropriateness and patient experience. Talk with the [RX2600 team](https://rx2600.com/) about the system and current program details before making projections.
