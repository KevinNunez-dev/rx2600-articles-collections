## Adding a tool, not replacing clinical judgment

Chiropractic practices may use a range of approaches to support their patients. The RX2600 Therapeutic Robot is a clinician-used tool for muscular therapy that practices can evaluate alongside their existing approach.

## Questions for your practice

Consider which patients and clinical goals the system may be appropriate for, how training and scheduling would work, and the requirements that apply in your jurisdiction. Discuss intended use and implementation with the RX2600 team.
