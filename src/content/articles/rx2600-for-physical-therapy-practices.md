## A tool within a larger plan

Physical therapy is built around individual assessment, clinical judgment, and a plan suited to each patient. The RX2600 Therapeutic Robot is used by clinicians as a tool in muscular therapy; it does not replace those essentials.

## Finding the right fit

Practices considering robotic therapy can assess how it might complement their existing services, workflow, and treatment goals. Training, patient selection, and scope of practice are important conversations to have before implementation.
