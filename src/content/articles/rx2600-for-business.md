## A new service starts with your practice

The RX2600 Therapeutic Robot is a tool used by clinicians in muscular therapy. For a practice exploring a new offering, the first question is how it could complement the care and services already provided.

## Consider the whole experience

Think about the needs of the people you serve, clinician training, available space, scheduling, and how the service would fit into existing workflows. Discuss implementation and current program terms with the RX2600 team before making a decision.
