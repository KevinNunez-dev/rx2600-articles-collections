## Start with the specific technology

Rehabilitation robotics is a broad term for technologies used in different care settings. Systems vary in their intended use, training requirements, and clinical applications, so a practice should evaluate each product on its own merits.

## Build a realistic business case

Identify the people you hope to serve, the service you plan to offer, the appointment capacity available, and the costs of introducing it. Model a range of demand levels rather than relying on a guaranteed increase in revenue.
